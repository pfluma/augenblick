Augenblick ist so gebaut, dass möglichst wenig über dich gespeichert wird — und nichts davon für Werbung. Hier steht ehrlich und konkret, was wir verarbeiten, warum, wie lange, und welche Rechte du hast. Sie gilt für die App und für unsere Website mit der Warteliste.

1. Wer verantwortlich ist

Verantwortlich für die Verarbeitung deiner Daten ist Michael Schnetzer, Arkenstraße 13, 6835 Muntlix, Österreich. Für alle Datenschutzanliegen erreichst du uns unter augenblick.app.team@gmail.com.

Einen Datenschutzbeauftragten haben wir nicht bestellt, weil wir dazu nach Art. 37 DSGVO derzeit nicht verpflichtet sind. Deine Anfragen beantworten wir selbst.

2. Unsere Grundsätze

Kein Live-Standort: Die App fragt nie nach der Standort-Berechtigung und erfasst nie, wo sich dein Gerät befindet. Orte wählst du immer selbst aus einer kuratierten Liste (z. B. „Stadtpark"). Augenblicke speichern bewusst nur ein grobes Zeitfenster („nachmittags"), nie eine Uhrzeit.

Keine Werbung, kein Tracking: Es gibt keine Werbe-SDKs, keine Analyse- oder Absturz-Tracker und keine Weitergabe von Daten zu Werbezwecken. Wir erstellen keine Profile über dein Verhalten.

Anonymität bis zur Gegenseitigkeit: Dein Name wird anderen erst sichtbar, wenn ihr beide eine Verbindung unabhängig voneinander bestätigt habt.

Keine Bewertungen von Menschen: Es gibt keine Sterne, keine Punkte und keine öffentlichen Kommentare über Personen.

Beim Anmelden protokolliert unser Hoster technisch bedingt die IP-Adresse und die Geräte- bzw. Browserkennung.

Wozu: um dein Konto zu führen, dich anzumelden, dir E-Mails zur Kontosicherheit zu schicken (z. B. Passwort zurücksetzen) und das Mindestalter von 18 Jahren durchzusetzen. Das Geburtsdatum sieht niemand außer dir; es wird nur zur Altersprüfung verwendet. Die Protokolle dienen der Sicherheit (z. B. um Angriffe auf Konten zu erkennen).

Rechtsgrundlage: Vertragserfüllung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO); für die Protokolle unser berechtigtes Interesse an einem sicheren Betrieb (lit. f).

5. Augenblicke und verdeckte Erinnerungs-Details

Was: der gewählte Ort, der Tag, das grobe Zeitfenster, deine Beschreibung des Moments und optional ein Erkennungsmerkmal, das dich selbst beschreibt. Diese Angaben sind für andere Nutzer:innen in deiner Stadt sichtbar — ohne deinen Namen.

Dazu kommen private Erinnerungs-Details, die du beim Festhalten und Beantworten angibst. Diese sieht niemand außer dir — nicht im Feed, nicht im Chat, auch nicht nach dem Kennenlernen. Der Server vergleicht die Details beider Seiten und speichert daraus nur einen Zahlenwert mit einer Stufe. Den Wert sehen auch die Beteiligten nie, nur die Moderation, und zwar ohne die Texte der jeweils anderen Seite.

Wozu: damit sich Menschen wiederfinden, die denselben Moment erlebt haben, und um Trittbrettfahrer:innen zu erschweren, die nur behaupten, dabei gewesen zu sein. Der Abgleich blockiert nie automatisch, er markiert Auffälliges für eine menschliche Prüfung (mehr in Abschnitt 17).

Wie lange: bis du den Augenblick oder dein Konto löschst.

6. Verbindungen, Chat und Treffen-Pläne

Was: der Status einer Verbindung zwischen zwei Augenblicken (bzw. einer QR-Verbindung), die Nachrichten, die ihr euch nach beidseitiger Bestätigung schreibt, und ein gemeinsamer Treffen-Plan, falls ihr einen macht: Ort, Tag, Uhrzeit und wer zugesagt hat. Die Uhrzeit im Treffen-Plan ist die einzige genaue Zeitangabe in der App — sie ist nur für euch beide sichtbar.

Wozu: damit ihr euch schreiben und verabreden könnt. Nachrichten und Pläne sind nur für die zwei Beteiligten lesbar; das erzwingt die Datenbank selbst, nicht nur die App.

Rechtsgrundlage: Vertragserfüllung (lit. b).

Wie lange: bis einer von euch sein Konto löscht. Löscht eine Person ihr Konto, verschwinden die Verbindung, alle Nachrichten und der Plan für beide — auch die Nachrichten der anderen Person. Das ist Absicht: Eine Unterhaltung zwischen zwei konkreten Menschen würde sonst weiter auf die gelöschte Person verweisen.

Augenblick is built so that as little as possible is stored about you, and none of it for advertising. This policy states honestly and concretely what we process, why, for how long, and which rights you have. It applies to the app and to our website with the waitlist.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for processing your data is Michael Schnetzer, Arkenstraße 13, 6835 Muntlix, Austria. For all privacy matters, you can reach us at augenblick.app.team@gmail.com.

We have not appointed a data protection officer because we are currently not required to under Art. 37 GDPR. We answer your requests ourselves.

2. Our principles

No live location: the app never asks for location permission and never records where your device is. You always choose places yourself from a curated list (for example “Stadtpark”). Missed moments deliberately store only a rough time window (“afternoon”), never a clock time.

No advertising, no tracking: there are no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash trackers, and no sharing of data for advertising. We do not build profiles of your behaviour.

Anonymity until it is mutual: your name only becomes visible to others once you have both confirmed a connection independently of each other.

No ratings of people: there are no stars, no points and no public comments about people.

3. Account and sign-in

What: email address, password (only as a cryptographic hash, we do not know it), an internal account ID, the time of registration, your date of birth and the server-set time at which you accepted this privacy policy and the terms of use. When you sign in, our host logs the IP address and the device or browser identifier for technical reasons.

Why: to run your account, sign you in, send you account security emails (for example password reset) and enforce the minimum age of 18. Nobody but you can see your date of birth; it is used only for the age check. The logs serve security (for example to detect attacks on accounts).

Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for the logs, our legitimate interest in secure operation (point (f)).

How long: until you delete your account. Security logs only for as long as our host keeps them technically. [TODO: Check the retention period of the Supabase logs (sign-in and access logs) for the plan in use and enter it here.]

4. Profile

What: first name or nickname, city, avatar colour, your visibility settings (for example invisible mode, visibility delay, who may reply to you) and an optional gender entry.

Why: so that the app works and your settings take effect on the server. The gender entry is used solely to enforce, on the server, areas that someone has opened “for women only”. It is never shown to others and is self-declared; we do not verify it.

Legal basis: performance of a contract (point (b)); for the gender entry, your consent (point (a)), which you can withdraw at any time by setting the entry in your profile to “no answer”.

How long: until you change the entry or delete your account.

5. Missed moments and hidden memory details

What: the place you chose, the day, the rough time window, your description of the moment and, optionally, a distinguishing feature that describes you. This information is visible to other users in your city, without your name.

In addition there are private memory details that you give when capturing and replying. Nobody but you can see these: not in the feed, not in the chat, not even after you have met. The server compares the details from both sides and stores only a number with a level from that. Even the people involved never see this value, only moderation does, and without the texts from the other side.

Why: so that people who experienced the same moment can find each other, and to make it harder for free riders who merely claim to have been there. The match never blocks automatically; it flags anything unusual for human review (more in section 17).

Legal basis: performance of a contract (point (b)); for the detail match, our legitimate interest (point (f)) in protecting users from deception, in particular the safety of women.

How long: until you delete the missed moment or your account.

6. Connections, chat and meet-up plans

What: the status of a connection between two missed moments (or a QR connection), the messages you write to each other after mutual confirmation, and a shared meet-up plan, if you make one: place, day, time and who has accepted. The time in the meet-up plan is the only exact time in the app, and it is visible only to the two of you.

Why: so that you can write to each other and arrange to meet. Messages and plans can only be read by the two people involved; the database itself enforces this, not just the app.

Legal basis: performance of a contract (point (b)).

How long: until one of you deletes their account. If one person deletes their account, the connection, all messages and the plan disappear for both, including the other person's messages. This is intentional: otherwise a conversation between two specific people would keep referring to the deleted person.

7. Connecting by QR code

What: when you use “Connect”, the server creates a one-time code (valid for three minutes) and stores who created it and who redeemed it. To scan, the app uses your device's camera, only with your permission and only to read the code. Camera images are processed solely on your device, not stored and not transmitted.

8. Tables, events and your places

Tables: tables you have opened, your join requests with a short introduction, and the answers in the mutual mini exchange. Only the table and you can see these.

Events: events you create, including the painted poster, and your sign-ups. Participant lists are not public; only the number is shown.

9. Invitations and real meetings

Invitations: every profile has an invitation code. If someone redeems your code, we store who invited whom. Only the two of you can see this; you can also see how many people joined through you. Anyone who knows your code sees your first name before registering (“You were invited by …”).

Real meetings: if you both confirm “We really met”, it counts as one real meeting for each of you. Only this number is visible: no names, no rating.

Legal basis: performance of a contract (point (b)). How long: until the account is deleted.

10. Safety: safety check, reporting, blocking, moderation

Private safety check: your answers after a meeting (“Did you feel safe?”). Apart from you, only moderation sees these, never the other person and never other users.

Reporting and blocking: we store whom you block so that the block takes effect everywhere. If you report someone, we store the reason, context and time; only moderation sees this. The person reported is not told who reported them.

Moderation: decisions such as a warning or suspension are logged with a timestamp. A suspended account can no longer create new content.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest (point (f)) in protecting users and in a safe service; where laws impose obligations on us (for example the Digital Services Act when handling reports), compliance with these obligations (point (c)). 16. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

Objection: where we rely on legitimate interests (point (f)), you can object on grounds relating to your particular situation. For safety functions (reports, moderation, detail match), we will then weigh the interests; the protection of other people may prevail.

You can exercise two rights directly in the app: “Export my data” gives you your data as a machine-readable file, and “Delete account” removes it permanently. For everything else, an informal email to the address in section 1 is enough. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at), or to the supervisory authority where you live or work in the EU.

17. No automated decision-making

There are no decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR). The hidden detail match calculates by a fixed, simple rule (word overlap and similarity of spelling), it does not learn from your data, and it is not artificial intelligence. It only produces a hint for human moderation and never blocks.

In principle, we store your data until you delete it or your account. You can trigger account deletion yourself at any time (Profile → “Delete account”); it removes your profile and content from the database immediately. Without access to the app, an email from your registered address is enough.

19. Data security

All connections are encrypted (TLS). Who may see what is enforced by the database itself with per-row access rules, not just by the app. Only expressly named people have moderation rights, and every moderation action is logged. Should a data breach happen anyway, we will report it to the data protection authority within 72 hours and inform you if there is a high risk for you (Art. 33 and 34 GDPR).

20. What is mandatory and what is voluntary

Without an email address, password, first name, city and date of birth, we cannot create an account. Everything else (gender entry, push, places, invitations, meet-up plans, feedback) is voluntary; the app also works without it.

Augenblick is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

21. Feedback on the test

What: if you send something under “Feedback on the test” in your profile, we store your text, the app version, the version of your operating system (for example “Android 14 (API 34)”) and the time. We do not store the device model or your IP address with it. The feedback is linked to your account so that it appears in your data export and is deleted with your account. It is displayed without your name.

Who sees it: only the people who look after Augenblick (moderation rights). The database itself enforces this. Other users never see it.

Why: to find errors and improve the app before and after launch. Sending feedback is voluntary; the app works just the same without it.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in an error-free, understandable app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Because you decide yourself whether and what to send, this hardly affects your interests.

How long: until we have evaluated it, at the latest six months after receipt, and earlier if you delete your account. Please do not write anything about your health or any details about other people in it. If something like that is in there anyway, we delete the entry as soon as we notice.
